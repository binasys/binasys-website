We follow the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA). This notice is also available in Bahasa Malaysia. If the two versions differ, the English version applies.

01 What we collect

When you send an enquiry through our contact form, we collect:

  • your name and email address
  • your company name and role
  • anything you write in your message

We also receive any details you choose to share when you email or call us. All fields on the form are required. If you leave them out, we may not be able to reply to you.

This website does not use cookies or analytics. Our hosting provider, GitHub Pages, may record technical details such as your IP address to keep the site running securely.

02 Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and arrange a conversation.
  • To prepare a proposal and deliver work you have agreed to.
  • To keep records we need for our business and to meet legal requirements.

We do not sell your personal data or use it for advertising.

03 Who we share it with

  • Formspree, the service that sends contact form messages to our inbox. Formspree is based in the United States, so your details are processed outside Malaysia.
  • The email and IT providers we use to run our business.
  • Authorities, where the law requires us to.

When your data is processed outside Malaysia, we use providers that apply appropriate safeguards to protect it.

04 How long we keep it

We keep your details only as long as we need them for the purposes above, or as long as the law requires. After that, we delete them.

05 How we protect it

We take practical steps to protect your personal data from loss, misuse and unauthorised access. Access is limited to the people at {{ business.name }} who need it.

06 Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • correct personal data that is wrong or out of date
  • stop using your personal data or delete it, where the law allows

We may charge a fee for access requests, as the PDPA allows. We will tell you before we do.

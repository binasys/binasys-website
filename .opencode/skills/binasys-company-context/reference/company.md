# Binasys — company overview

## In one sentence
Binasys provides hands-on business consulting for Malaysian SMEs, using business experience and AI to support strategy, analysis and implementation.

**Tagline:** Business, better.

## Key facts
- Founded: 2026
- Office: North Kiara, Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia
- Size: 3 co-founders
- Website: www.binasys.com.my
- GitHub: https://github.com/binasys

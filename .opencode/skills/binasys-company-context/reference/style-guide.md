# Binasys style guide

Source: Binasys brand guide (27 October 2026). For logos, colours and layouts in detail, use the `binasys-brand` skill.

## Tone of voice — a direct, useful voice
Describe the business problem, the work and the next step in plain English.

| Principle | Example |
|---|---|
| **Start with the business** | "We map the order process from quotation to payment." |
| **Name the actual work** | "We review records, find gaps and agree the changes." |
| **Be precise about progress** | "The draft is ready. The owner will confirm the next step." |

- Practical and grounded, never salesy or hyped. No buzzwords ("synergy", "leverage", "revolutionise", "cutting-edge").
- Talk about the client's business, not about AI for its own sake. AI is a tool that supports the work.
- Every update ends with a clear next step and who owns it.

## Writing rules
- Short sentences, plain words, active voice
- Lead with the point; details after
- Spelling: British English (colour, organise, programme)
- Dates: 27 October 2026 (day month year, no ordinal); short form 27 Oct 2026
- Currency: RM 1,200 (Malaysian ringgit)
- Company name is always written "Binasys" — never "BINASYS", "BinaSys" or "Binasys Sdn Bhd" unless the legal name is required. The lowercase "binasys" only appears inside the logo artwork.
- Tagline: "Building Tomorrow, Starting Today." (with the comma and full stop)

## Formatting
- Headings in sentence case
- Bullets for lists of 3+ items
- Tables: short wording, aligned columns, show owner and next step
- Numbered steps use two digits in brand material: 01, 02, 03

## Words to avoid / prefer
| Avoid | Prefer |
|---|---|
| Leverage, utilise | Use |
| Synergy, holistic solution | Name the actual work |
| Revolutionary, cutting-edge, game-changing | Describe the result plainly |
| "We're almost done" | "The draft is ready. <Owner> will confirm by <date>." |
| AI-powered everything | "We use AI to <specific task>" |

## Brand (summary)
- Primary colour: Binasys blue `#0066CC` (emphasis only — logo accents, links, one highlight)
- Text: near-black `#1D1D1F`; secondary `#626268`
- Backgrounds: white `#FFFFFF` (default), soft surface `#F5F5F7`; dividers `#E2E2E7`
- Font: system sans-serif on the website (Helvetica Neue / Arial for documents)
- Logos: primary wordmark "binasys" for introductions; compact "bns" for favicons and small placements. Files: `src/assets/brand/logos/`
